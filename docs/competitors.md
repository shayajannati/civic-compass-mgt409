# Comparable Products — Civic Compass

Checked 2026-10-26. "Unverified" means the page I fetched did not say.

## Comparison

| Product | What it does | Routes by issue to agencies? | Drafts letters? | Active? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Resistbot** | Chat-based tool that looks up your elected officials and delivers your message by email, fax, or mail. Also runs petitions and voter tools. | No. It targets elected officials (Congress, governors, state legislators, mayors). | Yes. It can draft a letter from a bill number or news article, or you write your own. | Yes. Site build dated Oct 2026, and petitions show recent signers. |
| **Democracy.io** (built for EFF) | Looks up your House member and two senators from your address and sends them your message. | No. Congress only. Commenting to agencies was listed as a future plan. | No. You write the message yourself. | Site loads and is maintained by Taskforce.is. Message delivery was not tested. Launched 2015. |
| **USA.gov "Find and contact elected officials"** | Static guide that links out to the White House, Senate, House, governors, mayors, and county officials. Also offers a call/chat contact center. | Partly. Contact center agents can point you to the right agency, but the page doesn't route by issue itself. | No. | Yes. |
| **Common Cause "Find Your Representative"** | Lookup by full address that shows your representatives' contact info, sponsored bills, committees, and political contributions. | No. | No (not mentioned). | Yes. |
| **5 Calls** | Nonprofit that has users pick an issue and call their member of Congress. Reports over 7 million calls. | No. Focused on Congress. | Call scripts were not confirmed on the fetched page (unverified). It doesn't write letters. | Yes. © 2026, with issues updated in October. |
| **Countable** | Was a consumer app for tracking bills and contacting representatives. Merged with Causes in 2020, with the consumer side moved to Causes.com and Countable relaunched as an enterprise advocacy platform. countable.us now redirects to Actionable, a paid self-service advocacy platform for organizations. | No. | Lawmaker messaging exists on Actionable for organizer-run campaigns. | The consumer Countable app appears discontinued (redirects). Causes.com is live (© 2026). A third-party source reports a 2024 acquisition by EV3 Global (unverified). |
| **ChatGPT** (general purpose) | General AI chatbot that can draft any letter and search the web. | No built-in routing directory. It can suggest offices, but answers can sound plausible and still be wrong (hallucination). | Yes, freely. | Yes. |
| **SeeClickFix 311 CRM** (CivicPlus) | Residents report local problems by app, web, chatbot, or phone. Requests land in a government inbox and are routed to departments such as Public Works and Parks. | Yes, but only within a city that has bought it. Local service requests only, no federal or state routing. | No. These are service requests, not letters. | Yes. Sold to local governments. |

## Takeaways

- Most tools (Resistbot, Democracy.io, 5 Calls, Common Cause, USA.gov) send people to **elected officials**, not to the **agency that actually handles the issue**.
- **SeeClickFix** is the only tool here that routes by issue to departments, and it works only inside a city that subscribes and only for local service requests.
- **ChatGPT** drafts well but has no verified agency directory and can make up contact details. Civic Compass's verified, source-dated directory fills that gap.
