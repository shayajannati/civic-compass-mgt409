# User study protocol: manual research vs. Civic Compass

**Status: PENDING. No participant has been run yet. Do not report results until real ones are recorded below.**

## Goal
Measure whether Civic Compass saves time and improves accuracy when a resident has to (a) find the government office responsible for a problem and (b) write a letter to it, compared with doing it by hand using a web browser and search engine.

## Participant
- One adult who does not work in government (for example a classmate, friend or family member). Record only a first name or initials.
- Verbal consent: "You will try two short tasks, about 25 minutes in total. I will time you and ask for feedback. Nothing you type will be stored by the app, and you can stop at any time."

## Materials
- A laptop with a browser. Civic Compass running at http://127.0.0.1:8765.
- A stopwatch (a phone is fine).
- Two scenario cards of similar difficulty (below). Counterbalance: show half of participants Scenario A first, and alternate which method they use first.

**Scenario A (housing).** "You rent an apartment in New Haven (ZIP 06511). Your landlord has not fixed your heat after three text messages over two weeks. Find the government office that can make the landlord fix it, find its contact details, and write a short letter asking for help."

**Scenario B (benefits).** "You live in New Haven (ZIP 06511). You applied for Social Security retirement benefits four months ago and have heard nothing. Find the government office that can help, find its contact details, and write a short letter asking for help."

## Procedure (per participant)
1. **Task 1, manual** (one scenario): browser and search only. No AI chatbots, so the baseline reflects current practice without AI. Start the timer when they start reading the card. Stop it when they say "done" with an office named and a letter written, or at 15 minutes.
2. **Task 2, Civic Compass** (the other scenario): start the timer at the same point. Stop it when the letter has been reviewed and copied.
3. After each task, ask the questions in the table below.
4. At the end, ask the two open questions and write down their answers verbatim.

## Scoring "correct office"
Score it before looking at the app's output for that scenario. Scenario A: New Haven Livable City Initiative (housing code enforcement). Scenario B: the Social Security Administration (with a member of Congress's casework office as an acceptable escalation). Score 1 if the participant named the right office **and** a working official contact method, 0.5 if they named the office but no contact method, and 0 otherwise.
